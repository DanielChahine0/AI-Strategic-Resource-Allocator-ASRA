D covers applicants aged roughly 65+ whose use case is everyday connection: video calls with family, email, banking, news, and government services. The appropriate match is a Tier 3 (Basic) computer or a Mobile item (tablet, phone). The rules layer permits T3 and Mobile for D; nothing more powerful is appropriate.

## Who qualifies

- Senior applicants applying on their own behalf.
- Caregivers applying on behalf of a senior — the `who_needs_it` field should reflect this.

## Typical use cases

- Video calls with family (FaceTime, WhatsApp, Zoom).
- Email and basic web browsing.
- Online banking and bill payment.
- Government and benefits portals (CRA, ServiceCanada, provincial health portals).
- Reading news, light document writing.

## Tier rationale

T3 is universally adequate for this workload. Many D applicants are better served by a tablet (Mobile) than a laptop — larger touch targets, simpler interaction. Match a Mobile if the applicant's `main_usage` is video calls plus reading; match a T3 laptop if there is significant typing (banking forms, government applications, document drafting).

## Edge cases

- Applicants caring for grandchildren who also use the device may push share-count to ≥3; the priority bump applies but the tier choice remains T3 or Mobile.
- Accessibility needs (low vision, motor difficulty) are not in the 7-question intake — flag for LGT staff review and consider bundling assistive peripherals.
