B covers applicants whose primary need is engaging with the healthcare system: virtual appointments, patient portals, prescription management, and accessing records. Per LGT direct-linking rules, B applicants receive Tier 2 (Standard) or Tier 3 (Basic) — never Tier 1.

## Who qualifies

- Patients managing a chronic condition who need reliable access to a portal (MyChart, Connect Care, equivalent).
- Caregivers managing appointments on behalf of a family member.
- Applicants whose mental-health treatment relies on virtual therapy.

## Typical use cases

- Joining video appointments (Zoom, OTN, equivalent telehealth platforms).
- Logging into provincial patient portals to view results, book appointments, request prescription renewals.
- Reading and printing prescription information; filling out intake forms.
- Communicating with care teams by secure messaging.

## Tier rationale

T3 covers every workload above cleanly. T2 is offered when there's a secondary use (job searching, education) that the same applicant doesn't qualify for separately. T1 is explicitly disallowed — the workload doesn't require it and a T1 in this slot starves a software-engineering student.

## Edge cases

- Vision impairment or other accessibility needs may shift the choice (e.g., larger display preferred). Accessibility is not currently captured in the 7-question intake; flag for staff review.
- Applicants who are also job-searching: the splitter creates a [B, C] pair and keeps the stronger match.
