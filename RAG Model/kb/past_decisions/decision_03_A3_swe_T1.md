A second-year software engineering student needed a primary device. Their courses required them to run Docker locally for a web-services class and to test on an Android emulator for a mobile course. They were sharing a friend's laptop. We allocated a T1 laptop (i7, 16 GB, SSD with discrete GPU, condition 4/5) available within three weeks.

## Score breakdown

- priority 0.75 (high urgency + shared device)
- timing 0.90
- condition 0.80
- efficiency 1.00 (T1 matches the workload exactly)
- composite 0.86

## Justification

Docker Desktop and Android Studio emulators are the two clearest T1 justifications in the capability matrix. Allocating a T2 here would have left the student unable to complete coursework, and the engine correctly chose T1 over the more abundant T2 inventory. The decision is auditable via the citation chain to the software capability matrix.
