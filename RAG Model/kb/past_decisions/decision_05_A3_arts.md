A college student in graphic design needed Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign for daily classwork. They were using an outdated laptop. We allocated a T2 laptop (Apple M1, 16 GB, condition 4/5) available within two weeks.

## Score breakdown

- priority 0.75
- timing 0.93
- condition 0.80
- efficiency 1.00 (T2 matches the workload)
- composite 0.85

## Justification

Photoshop and Illustrator run cleanly on T2 — the capability matrix lists both at minimum tier T2. Premiere Pro was not in the student's software list; if it had been, the recommendation would have shifted to T1.
