Tier 2 (Standard) is the workhorse of the LGT inventory. It runs everything a university student, office worker, designer, or remote worker needs to be productive. Most A3 and many C allocations land here. T2 is the right answer for the majority of computer-needing applicants.

## Representative specs

- 4-core CPU (Intel i5, AMD Ryzen 5, Apple M1/M2).
- 8 GB RAM minimum, 16 GB preferred.
- 256 GB SSD or larger.
- Integrated graphics are fine for this tier.

## Workloads T2 handles cleanly

- Office documents, slides, spreadsheets — Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Google Workspace.
- Photoshop, Illustrator, Figma, Audition.
- VS Code with language servers and a handful of extensions; introductory web/scripting.
- MATLAB, RStudio, Jupyter on small/medium datasets.
- Multiple Chrome tabs, Zoom calls, screen sharing.
- AutoCAD lite use, accounting software, Tableau.

## When to step up to T1

If the applicant's `software_needed` includes VMs, Docker Desktop, Android Studio, Xcode, Unity, Unreal, or local ML training (PyTorch/TensorFlow with GPU), T2 will struggle and T1 should be considered.

## When to step down to T3

If the applicant's intake reveals only browser- and document-based workloads with no mentioned software, T3 is sufficient and preferred. Confirm before stepping down — applicants often forget to mention software they use weekly.
