Tier 3 (Basic) is the essential-access tier. It is fully sufficient for web browsing, email, document writing, video calls, and government services — the workloads that define the A1, D, E, F categories and most B and C applicants. T3 is the most abundant inventory in the LGT pool.

## Representative specs

- 2- or 4-core CPU (Intel i3, older i5, AMD Ryzen 3, Apple M-series base).
- 4–8 GB RAM.
- 128–256 GB SSD or eMMC; Chromebooks acceptable.
- Integrated graphics.

## Workloads T3 handles cleanly

- Web browsing, email, social media, video streaming.
- A single Zoom or Google Meet call at a time.
- Microsoft 365 web app and Google Workspace.
- Light document writing, slides, spreadsheets.
- Government and healthcare portals (CRA, ServiceCanada, MyChart equivalents).

## Workloads T3 will struggle with

- Multiple Zoom calls plus a dozen browser tabs simultaneously.
- Photoshop, Illustrator (use Tier 2 instead).
- VS Code with multiple extensions on large projects.
- Spreadsheets with tens of thousands of rows and complex formulas.
- Any video editing, 3D work, virtualization, or local ML.

## When NOT to allocate T3

If the applicant's intake includes any T2 or T1 software in the capability matrix, T3 violates the fit gate and the engine should not match it.
